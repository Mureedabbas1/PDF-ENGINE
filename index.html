Frequently asked questions

Is PDF Engine really free?

Yes. All 20 tools are free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limits.

Do you upload my files to a server?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser. Your supported files are processed in your browser, which keeps them private.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. PDF Engine is fully responsive and works on modern phones, tablets and computers.

Which browsers are supported?

The latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Brave work great.

Is there a file size limit?

We do not set a limit. Very large files depend on your device memory, so older phones may need more time.

Will my PDF quality change?

Merge, split, rotate, page numbers and watermark keep the original quality. Compress trades some quality for a smaller size, and you choose the level.
