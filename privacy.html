Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy describes the current PDF Engine website and browser-based tools.

PDF Engine is an online service used directly in your web browser, not a downloadable desktop software product. Files downloaded from a tool are document, image, or other task-result outputs, not software installers. The website does not distribute a desktop application, installer, browser extension, or plugin.

Files you select

Supported operations are designed to process selected files in your browser. This static website does not currently provide server-side file upload or account storage. Files are not intentionally sent to a PDF Engine document-processing server. Some file types, encrypted PDFs, damaged files, and very large documents may not work. Keep original copies and inspect every result before relying on it.

Third-party resources and connection information

The site loads JavaScript libraries and fonts from third-party content delivery networks. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address, browser type and request information. Those providers handle this data under their own privacy policies.

Local storage and cookies

This version may store a light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. Google Analytics 4 is configured on the main page (measurement ID G-K1RMFHCTEP), and the Google AdSense script and publisher meta tag are included on the site. Whether ads are actually served depends on Google’s review and serving status. Local storage is controlled by your browser and can be cleared in browser settings.

Advertising cookies and choices

The Google AdSense script is present on the site. If Google serves ads, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalize and measure ads. Third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings, or review industry opt-out choices at aboutads.info. For users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the publisher must ensure a Google-certified consent management platform is configured where required before serving ads.

Children

This general-purpose utility website is not designed specifically for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when site features, analytics or advertising change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or corrections, use the PDF Engine GitHub issue tracker. Please do not include private personal information in a public issue.
