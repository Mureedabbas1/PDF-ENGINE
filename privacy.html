Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

PDF Engine provides browser-based file utilities. This page describes the current static website and may need updating if additional services are introduced.

Files you select

Supported operations are designed to process selected files in your browser. This website does not currently provide a server-side file upload or account-storage feature. Some operations rely on third-party JavaScript libraries delivered by content delivery networks (CDNs); those providers may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address when your browser requests library files. Do not use the service for sensitive documents unless you are comfortable with this design.

Preferences and cookies

The site may use your browser's local storage to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice. This version does not include Google Analytics or Google AdSense code. If analytics, advertising, or other tracking services are added, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before launch.

Third-party services

External font and JavaScript resources may be loaded from their respective providers. Their handling of connection data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions or corrections, open an issue in the PDF Engine GitHub repository.
