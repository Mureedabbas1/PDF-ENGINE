About Rotate PDF

Scanned documents often come out sideways. With this tool you can rotate the whole file or just specific pages, and the new orientation is saved inside the PDF so it looks right in every viewer.

When it is useful

Correct sideways scans, upside-down pages or mixed-orientation documents before printing or sharing.

Important limitations

Check pages with mixed orientations carefully. Rotation changes page orientation metadata and does not re-render page content.

Privacy and file handling

This tool is designed to process supported files locally in your browser; the static website does not provide server-side file storage. Your browser still downloads the JavaScript libraries required for some operations from third-party content delivery networks. See the Privacy Policy for details. Do not use the output without checking it, and keep your original file.

Good to know

Rotate PDF Pages: A Practical Guide

Rotating pages corrects sideways or upside-down documents, especially scans assembled from different sources. It makes a PDF easier to read on screen and more convenient to print when portrait and landscape pages are mixed.

Choose the correct direction

Check whether a page needs a clockwise quarter-turn, a half-turn or the opposite quarter-turn. A landscape page may already be correct, so avoid rotating every page unless the whole document needs the same adjustment.

Rotate and verify

Select the PDF, choose the available rotation and process the file. Review every page, including tables, signatures and diagrams. Confirm that text is upright and that the page fits comfortably in the viewer.

Rotation is not deskewing

Rotating changes a page's orientation. It does not necessarily straighten a crooked scan or improve image quality. A tilted image may need a separate deskew or image-editing workflow.

Frequently asked question

Will rotation change my original PDF? The tool creates an output copy. Keep the original until the rotated version has been checked in a PDF reader and print preview.

Common questions about Rotate PDF

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Type the page number in the Pages box, for example 3, or a range like 2-5.

Is the rotation saved permanently?

Yes. The downloaded file has the new page orientation saved inside it.

Will rotating reduce quality?

No. Pages are only turned, not re-rendered, so quality stays the same.
