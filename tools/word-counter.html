About Word Counter

Writers, students and marketers often need to stay within a word limit for essays, social posts, meta descriptions and articles. This counter updates as you type and also shows estimated reading and speaking time and your most used keywords.

When it is useful

Check essay word limits, article length, social posts, meta descriptions or short writing assignments.

Important limitations

Counting rules can differ for hyphenated words, languages and unusual punctuation. Treat reading and speaking times as estimates.

Word Counter: A Practical Guide

A word counter helps check writing length for essays, applications, reports, product descriptions and social posts. It is useful for planning and editing, but different publishers and institutions may apply their own counting rules.

Count your draft

Paste or type your content into the text area. Review the word and character totals as you edit. Before using the count, remove accidental duplicated paragraphs and check that the final version contains all headings and captions that should be included.

Words and characters are different

A general word count often treats groups separated by whitespace as words. Character counts may include or exclude spaces depending on the metric. URLs, hyphenated terms, contractions, punctuation and some languages may be counted differently by other systems.

Meet a strict word limit

If a submission has a maximum length, leave room for headings, citations and captions when the rules include them. For an official submission, follow the counting method specified by the school, employer, journal or platform.

Frequently asked question

Is this an official word-count validator? No. It is a general writing aid. The receiving organisation's rules take priority.

Common questions about Word Counter

How is reading time calculated?

We assume an average adult reads about 238 words per minute.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Your text stays in your browser and is never sent to a server.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. It shows both characters with spaces and characters without spaces.
