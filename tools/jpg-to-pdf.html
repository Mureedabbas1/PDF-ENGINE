JPG to PDF: A Practical Guide

Converting JPG images into a PDF is useful for combining receipts, photographed notes, scanned pages and document snapshots into one file. A single PDF can be easier to email, print and archive than a collection of separate image attachments.

Prepare images in the right order

Select clear images and arrange them in the sequence you want readers to follow. Check that pages are upright, text is readable and important content is not cut off. Crop distracting borders before conversion if needed.

Create and check the PDF

Select the supported image files, confirm their order and run the conversion. Download the PDF and scroll through every page. Check for missing images, unexpected rotation, stretching and margins that make text difficult to read.

Page size and quality

Image dimensions and aspect ratios affect how each image fits on a PDF page. Small images may look blurry when enlarged, while high-resolution photos can create a large PDF. Resize or compress copies when an upload limit matters, but retain the originals.
