Age Calculator: A Practical Guide

An age calculator measures the time between a date of birth and a selected reference date. It can be useful when completing a form, checking a milestone or calculating an age on a past or future date. The result depends on the exact dates, month lengths and leap years.

Enter dates carefully

Enter the date of birth and, if supported, choose the date on which the age should be measured. Otherwise the calculator uses the current date. Check the day, month and year format before calculating; formats such as 04/05/2020 can be interpreted differently in different regions.

Understand years, months and days

Completed years are not simply the difference between calendar years. A person does not reach the next age until their birthday. The remaining months and days depend on the exact dates and the lengths of the calendar months involved.

Official eligibility rules may differ

This calculator provides a date calculation, not an official legal or eligibility decision. School admission cut-offs, insurance rules, benefits and legal age requirements may use specific local rules. Confirm those rules with the organisation requesting the information.

Common questions about Age Calculator

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the Age at the date of field to any date after the date of birth.

How are months and days counted?

The calculator uses real calendar months, so leap years and different month lengths are handled correctly.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is saved.
