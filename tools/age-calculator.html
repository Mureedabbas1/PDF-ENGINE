How to use Age Calculator

  1. Choose the date of birth.
  2. Choose the date to calculate age at, or keep the default date.
  3. Read the age in years, months, days and total time units.

When it is useful

Get a quick calendar-age estimate for forms, planning and everyday calculations.

Important limitations

This is a convenience calculator, not proof of legal eligibility. Official requirements may define age and dates differently by jurisdiction.

Privacy and file handling

This tool is designed to process supported files locally in your browser; the static website does not provide server-side file storage. Your browser still downloads the JavaScript libraries required for some operations from third-party content delivery networks. See the Privacy Policy for details. Do not use the output without checking it, and keep your original file.

Frequently asked question

The calculator uses calendar months and dates; check official rules for legal, school or employment eligibility.
