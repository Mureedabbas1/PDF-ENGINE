About Crop PDF

Crop boxes hide margins but do not erase underlying content.

When it is useful

Make oversized margins less distracting or standardize the visible page area for reading and printing.

Important limitations

Cropping changes the crop box; it does not permanently erase content outside the visible area. Do not use it as secure redaction.

Privacy and file handling

This tool is designed to process supported files locally in your browser; the static website does not provide server-side file storage. Your browser still downloads the JavaScript libraries required for some operations from third-party content delivery networks. See the Privacy Policy for details. Do not use the output without checking it, and keep your original file.

Good to know

Keep the original if hidden content must be removed rather than merely hidden.

Crop PDF Pages: A Practical Guide

Cropping changes the visible page area. It can remove oversized margins from scans, reduce unused whitespace or create a cleaner page presentation. It is useful for scanned handouts, receipts and documents where the content sits inside a large border.

Check the page layout first

Review portrait and landscape pages before choosing a crop. A single crop amount may not fit every page in a mixed document. Look for footnotes, page numbers, stamps, handwritten notes and signatures near the edges.

Crop and inspect the result

Select the PDF, choose the available crop settings and process a copy. Open the output and inspect pages with the widest and narrowest content areas. Confirm that no important information has been cut off.

Cropping is not redaction

Hiding content outside the visible page boundary does not guarantee that the underlying information has been permanently removed. Do not crop out confidential details as a security measure. Use a dedicated redaction workflow and verify the result.

Frequently asked question

Can I restore the original page area? Keep the source PDF. The cropped output may not be a convenient way to recover the original boundaries.
