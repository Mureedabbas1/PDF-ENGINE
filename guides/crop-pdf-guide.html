Crop PDF: A Practical Guide

Cropping a PDF can make pages cleaner by reducing empty margins or removing unwanted edges from scans. It is important to distinguish cropping from deleting content: depending on the method, content outside the visible crop may still exist in the file.

When to use Crop PDF

  • Trim excessive margins around scanned pages.
  • Improve the presentation of a chart or embedded page.
  • Make a consistent page layout for review or printing.

How to use the Crop PDF tool

  1. Open Crop PDF and select the source file.
  2. Choose the crop area or margin settings available in the tool.
  3. Apply the crop and download the revised PDF.
  4. Inspect every page type, especially pages with footnotes, signatures, or content near the edges.

Tips for a better result

  • Use a test copy before cropping a long document.
  • Leave enough room for page numbers and footnotes.
  • Do not treat cropping as secure removal of sensitive content.
