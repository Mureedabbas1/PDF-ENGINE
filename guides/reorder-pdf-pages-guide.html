Reorder PDF Pages: A Practical Guide

A scanned or assembled PDF can contain pages in the wrong order. Reordering helps restore a logical flow before a document is read, printed, or submitted.

When to use Reorder PDF Pages

  • Correct pages scanned in a mixed sequence.
  • Move an appendix or cover page to the right position.
  • Organize a portfolio or application packet.

How to use the Reorder PDF Pages tool

  1. Open Reorder PDF Pages and upload the source PDF.
  2. Review the page thumbnails or page positions to identify the intended sequence.
  3. Move pages into the correct order and process the file.
  4. Download the result and read through the whole document to confirm the sequence.

Tips for a better result

  • Write down the desired sequence first for long documents.
  • Check page numbers printed on the page as well as the viewer's page index.
  • Review bookmarks and table-of-contents references after reordering.

Limitations and privacy

For sensitive documents, follow your organization's data-handling rules.

Frequently asked question

Reordering changes the sequence of pages in the output PDF. It may not automatically update printed page numbers, bookmarks, or references inside the document.
