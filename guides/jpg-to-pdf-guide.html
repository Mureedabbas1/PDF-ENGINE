JPG to PDF: A Practical Guide

Converting JPG images to PDF helps keep several photos or scanned pages together in one document. It is useful for school assignments, application paperwork, receipts, travel documents, and image-based reports.

When to use JPG to PDF

  • Combine photographed pages into one document for an online application.
  • Keep receipts and records in a single file for easier archiving.
  • Prepare image pages for printing or emailing as one attachment.

How to use the JPG to PDF tool

  1. Put images in the order they should appear, and rotate any sideways photos first.
  2. Open the JPG to PDF tool and select the supported image files from your device.
  3. Check the preview or file order, then create and download the PDF.
  4. Open the downloaded PDF and verify that every page is legible and correctly oriented.

Tips for a better result

  • Use clear, well-lit images; a PDF cannot restore detail missing from a blurry photo.
  • Crop unnecessary borders before converting if they make text too small.
  • Keep the original images until the PDF has been checked.
