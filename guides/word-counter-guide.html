Word Counter: A Practical Guide

A word counter helps writers check whether text fits an assignment, form, article, social post, or application limit. Character counts can matter as much as word counts when a platform limits the length of a field.

When to use Word Counter

  • Check an essay against a word limit.
  • Measure a product description or article draft.
  • Check character length for forms and short social posts.

How to use the Word Counter tool

  1. Open Word Counter and paste or type your text into the text area.
  2. Review the displayed word and character totals.
  3. Edit the text and check the count again before copying it into the destination.
  4. Read the final text for clarity; a correct count does not replace proofreading.

Tips for a better result

  • Confirm whether the destination counts words, characters with spaces, or characters without spaces.
  • Hyphenated terms and contractions can be counted differently by different systems.
  • Keep a copy of longer drafts before experimenting with edits.
